# QFedAutoML Literature Review

## Foundations & References

### 1. Federated Learning
- McMahan, B., Moore, E., Ramage, D., Hampson, S., & y Arcas, B. A. (2017). *Communication-Efficient Learning of Deep Networks from Decentralized Data*. AISTATS.
- Li, T., Sahu, A. K., Zaheer, M., Sanjabi, M., Talwalkar, A., & Smith, V. (2020). *Federated Optimization in Heterogeneous Networks (FedProx)*. MLSys.

### 2. Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA)
- Farhi, E., Goldstone, J., & Gutmann, S. (2014). *A Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm*. arXiv:1411.4028.
- Lucas, A. (2014). *Ising formulations of many NP problems*. Frontiers in Physics, 2, 5.

### 3. Differential Privacy in Machine Learning
- Abadi, M., Chu, A., Goodfellow, I., McMahan, H. B., Mironov, I., Talwar, K., & Zhang, L. (2016). *Deep Learning with Differential Privacy*. ACM CCS.

### 4. Explainable AI
- Lundberg, S. M., & Lee, S. I. (2017). *A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions (SHAP)*. NeurIPS.
- Ribeiro, M. T., Singh, S., & Guestrin, C. (2016). *"Why Should I Trust You?": Explaining the Predictions of Any Classifier (LIME)*. ACM KDD.
